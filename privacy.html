HIREDRN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Summary

HiredRN helps you practice job interviews. We collect as little as possible, we never sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect

• An anonymous user ID created when you first open the app (no name, email or phone number is required).

• The text of your interview answers, the questions asked, your chosen role, interview settings and any job description you paste, while you use the AI interviewer.

• How many interviews you start each day, so we can apply plan limits.

• Purchase status for HiredRN Pro, provided by Apple through our subscription provider RevenueCat.

What we do not collect

We do not record or store audio. Speech is converted to text on your iPhone by Apple’s speech recognition. We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps or websites.

How we use AI

With your permission, the text described above is sent through our server to Anthropic, PBC (Claude AI) to generate interview questions, scores and feedback. Anthropic processes this data to provide the service to us. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings; the AI interviewer will then be unavailable.

Where your data is stored

Your interview history is stored on your device. Our server (hosted by Supabase in the United States) stores your anonymous ID and daily usage counts. Answer text sent to the AI is processed to produce a response and is not stored in our database.

Your choices

Use “Delete all my data” in Settings to erase the interview history on your device and your usage records on our server. You can also contact us to make a request under applicable privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Health information

HiredRN is not a medical service. Please do not include patient names or other protected health information in your answers.

Children

HiredRN is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Contact us at the support email shown in Settings.
