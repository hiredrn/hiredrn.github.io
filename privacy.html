HIREDRN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Summary

HiredRN helps you practice job interviews. We collect as little as possible, we never sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the interview practice, and only in these ways:

• An anonymous user ID, created automatically when you first open the app. No name, email or phone number is needed.

• Interview content you type or dictate in the app: your answers, the questions asked, your chosen role and settings, and any job description you paste. You provide it directly; it is not collected in the background.

• Daily interview and AI-request counts, recorded by our server so we can apply plan limits.

• Purchase status for HiredRN Pro, provided by Apple through our subscription provider RevenueCat.

What we do not collect

We do not record or store audio. Speech is converted to text on your iPhone by Apple’s speech recognition. We do not use advertising identifiers or track you across other apps or websites.

How we use AI

This is the only third party that receives your interview content. Only after you tap Allow, the interview content listed above is sent through our server to Anthropic, PBC, a US AI company (Claude AI). We send nothing else: no name, email, contacts, photos or audio. Anthropic uses it only to generate interview questions, scores and feedback for you. We do not sell it or use it for advertising, and Anthropic does not use it to train its models. Our agreement requires Anthropic to protect your data with the same or equal protection as described in this policy, and it may keep it for a limited time under its own policy (anthropic.com/privacy). If you tap Don’t Allow, or turn permission off later in Settings, nothing is sent and the AI interviewer is unavailable.

How we use your data

We use your data only to (1) run the AI interviewer and show your reports, (2) apply daily limits and check Pro status, and (3) keep the service secure. We do not use it for advertising, tracking or profiling, and we do not sell or share it for other purposes.

Where your data is stored

Your interview history is stored on your device. Our server (hosted by Supabase in the United States) stores your anonymous ID and daily usage counts. Answer text sent to the AI is processed to produce a response and is not stored in our database.

Your choices

Use “Delete all my data” in Settings to erase the interview history on your device and your usage records on our server. You can also email hiredrn@proton.me to ask us to access or delete your data, including a request under applicable privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Health information

HiredRN is not a medical service. Please do not include patient names or other protected health information in your answers.

Children

HiredRN is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Contact us at the support email shown in Settings.
